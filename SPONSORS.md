# Support Personal Learning Agent

Personal Learning Agent is an experimental local-first Windows AI companion focused on memory, useful workflow assistance, safe desktop automation, and accessible personal computing.

## How support would be used

Voluntary sponsorships, once enabled, would support real-device Windows testing, CPU and compatible GPU benchmarking, accessible companion design, documentation, and privacy/security reviews. Funding does not grant access to anyone's computer or private files.

### Become a sponsor

- [GitHub Sponsors profile for 10GODS](https://github.com/sponsors/10GODS) — **this payment option may be unavailable until the maintainer completes GitHub's account approval and payout setup**.
- [Follow and star the repository](https://github.com/10GODS/PersonalLearningAgent-Windows) — free and genuinely helpful.
- [Describe the next feature you would like to see](https://github.com/10GODS/PersonalLearningAgent-Windows/issues/new) — feedback helps determine priorities.

**Support is optional.** Do not send payments via an unverified third-party account or share passwords, payment details, personal datasets, screenshots, or account credentials with someone claiming to represent this project.

### Potential supporter benefits — not yet active

Possible future sponsorship tiers could offer periodic development notes, voting on non-security feature priorities, or early access to **publicly documented** preview builds. Any tier and benefit will be shown on the activated sponsorship profile before payments are accepted. Sponsorship does not guarantee a feature, response time, bug fix, commercial license, or direct support unless explicitly stated in an active tier.

### Partner or organizational support

Organizations interested in funding Windows device testing, accessibility work, or an approved workflow pilot can open an issue describing the proposal without confidential information. The maintainer can discuss a separate scope and contract if appropriate.

**Project status:** prototype. The public repository does not currently distribute the complete application source or a Windows installer. Financial support is for development, not a purchase of a finished product.
